Hello, I’m Andrew, a product UX and data-visualization designer who has spent the past decade turning dense, technical information into experiences that feel effortless. I work at the crossroads of design and data, pairing deep user research with the science of visual perception, creating user-centered tools that help users explore data to discover insights, uncover trends, and enable data-driven decisions.

Throughout my career, I’ve focused on applying design thinking and visual methods to simplify complexity, reduce cognitive load, and clarify information. I thrive in technical environments: digging into workflows, identifying pain points, and designing solutions that make systems more intuitive and efficient. Thoughtful, purposeful user experiences are always at the heart of my work.

Though my roots are in life-science products, I’m energized by any complex, technical domain where clear design can drive better decisions. I carry the same research-driven rigor and user empathy that have served me in science-based workflows into finance, climate tech, and other data-heavy arenas.

Today I consult with multiple teams, shaping and prototyping 0→1 products while auditing their digital processes end-to-end. By mapping workflows, surfacing friction points, and translating UX insights into business terms, I help organizations uncover efficiencies and steer smarter product choices.

Across every challenge, my goal remains constant: use principled design and data visualization to simplify complexity so people can think, decide, and discover, then get out of their way.
