I’m Andrew, a product design leader working at the intersection of design and data. For nearly a decade I’ve turned dense, technical information into tools people can think with, from cancer-research data portals at the Broad Institute to enterprise software for synthetic biology at Ginkgo Bioworks and AI-assisted manufacturing review at Moderna.

I start with research, auditing digital processes end-to-end: mapping workflows, surfacing friction points, and translating UX insights into business terms. Then I shape and prototype 0→1 products along with stakeholders and end users. My process is agentified, so I go quickly from idea to working prototype and test assumptions with real software instead of static mockups.

Today I’m an Associate Director of Design Foundations at Novartis, designing human-in-the-loop agentic workflows that bring AI into everyday business decisions. My goal hasn’t changed: simplify complexity so people can think, decide, and discover, then get out of their way.
